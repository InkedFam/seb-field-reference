SHERIFF'S 24

RULERS OF THE NIGHT

The Special Enforcement Bureau shall provide a variety of specialized tactical, rescue, and counter-terrorism support services to Sheriff's Department personnel and the County. The primary goal of SEB is to save lives. As such, its expertise is in solving dangerous situations through experience, training, and the use of highly specialized weapons and equipment.
